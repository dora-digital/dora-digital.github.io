01 About

From Aviation to IT

Dorathy Godwin Ogbuh Dora Digital

A career built around systems, people and problem-solving.

Before IT, I spent four years as a flight attendant, a job built entirely on procedure, checklists, and staying composed when a flight doesn't go to plan. That mindset transferred more directly to IT than I expected. Troubleshooting a network fault isn't so different from working through a pre-flight checklist under pressure. You follow the process, you don't skip steps, and you stay calm while you find the cause.

I started my Umschulung (retraining) to become a Fachinformatikerin für Systemintegration in January 2026, and I'm documenting the whole transition publicly as Dora Digital, partly to hold myself accountable and partly because I know other career-changers are asking the same questions I had a year ago.

🧩 Structured troubleshooting

Trained to isolate a fault by checking the most likely cause first, not guessing randomly. Applied directly in my Cisco Packet Tracer labs and DHCP/DNS troubleshooting exercises.

✈️ Working under real pressure

Four years handling in-flight situations taught me the difference between "urgent" and "actually critical", a skill that matters in IT support triage.
